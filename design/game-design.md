## The Puzzle

- Every day there is one 6 x 6 grid of letters.
- A word is traced through touching squares (across, up and down, or corner to corner), using each square at most
  once. Words must be 4+ letters and in the word list (SCOWL size 50, American English: no proper nouns, apostrophes,
  hyphens or blocked words).
- Every square a found word went through is **lit**. The goal is to light all 36. Every grid is made so that this is
  possible, but 2-5 squares are reached by hardly any everyday words.
- A word already found can be traced again along different squares, if that lights at least one new square (no extra
  points).
- The day changes at local midnight (in family play, the family's timezone).

## Scoring and Ranks

- Ranks come from the squares lit: Dark 0, Flicker 1+, Glimmer 25%, Glow 50%, Shine 70%, Radiant 85%, All Aglow 100%.
- Words also score points (Boggle-style): 1 for 4 letters, 2 for 5, 3 for 6, 5 for 7, 11 for 8+.
- Lighting every square shows a celebration; other rank-ups show a short message.

## Playing

- Drag through the letters and let go to submit; or tap letters one at a time (tapping a letter in the path goes back
  to it) and tap the last again, or press Enter. While dragging, only the middle of each square counts, so diagonal
  moves don't pick up the squares beside the way.
- Squares glow brighter the more found words go through them.
- **Hints** (the bulb): each dark square shows how many words not yet found go through it. Asks first; the family can
  see who used hints.
- The found-words box shows how many of the grid's words are found, and opens to the list.

## Screens

- **Welcome** (every visit), **Rankings** (tap the progress bar), **Yesterday's Answers** (the grid, what was lit, and
  every answer), **Compare** (family play), **How to Play**, **Menu** (theme, about with the GitHub link, erase local
  progress), and the family screens (shared with the other games).

## Family Play

- **Who you're playing with:** the button above the progress bar. Families are shared with Buzz Off and the other
  games.
- **Everyone plays on their own:** the same grid, each with their own words, lit squares and rank.
- **Compare** (the trophy): each member's squares lit, words, points, longest word (others' only as its length until
  combined) and whether they used hints.
- **Combine our work:** asks first, then lights everyone's words on the board together: each square shows a dot for
  everyone who lit it, each word a dot for its first finder, and Compare adds the family's total ("Together"). Words
  found while combined still count for the player. Entering a word someone else found says "Already found (by Mom)".
  "Show only mine" goes back. The choice is kept for the day, on the device.
